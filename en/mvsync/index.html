MVsync {% include mvsync-logo.svg %}

Side-by-side video comparison app for Android

Line up two videos to the exact moment and play them together frame by frame. Built for checking sports form, comparing swings, and reviewing before/after footage.

Google Play (Coming Soon)

Key Features

Line Up & Play Together

Move either video with its seek bar or frame step controls to match key moments like impact or takeoff. On supported devices, both videos stay synced frame for frame.

Up to 8x Pinch Zoom

Zoom and pan each video independently with two fingers so you can focus closely on details like wrist angle or footwork side by side.

Frame Step, Speed & Range Loop

Step cleanly frame by frame, adjust playback speed from 0.25x to 2x, and set a start/end range to loop only the moments that matter.

Fullscreen Playback Mode

Once your timing and range are dialed in, expand both videos edge to edge. Top/bottom or side-by-side layout automatically adapts to video orientation and screen size.

Save & Restore Comparisons

Save your selected videos, timing, range, and zoom under a custom name to revisit later, and automatically restore your previous session when reopening the app.

Free vs. Premium

Free

  • Exported videos and images include a small watermark
  • Core comparison, playback, and save features are included

MVsync Premium (One-time purchase)

  • Unlimited simultaneous playback time
  • No ads at all
  • No watermark on exported videos or images

Privacy & Requirements

  • On-device processing: Videos you pick stay on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Requirements: Android 7.0 or later
