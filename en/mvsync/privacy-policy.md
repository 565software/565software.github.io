# Privacy Policy

MVsync (565software)

## Introduction

This policy describes how the Android app "MVsync" (the "App") handles user information.

## Data Collection

The developer of the App does not collect any personal information. No analytics SDKs are used.

However, the free version of the App shows ads, and uses Google's advertising service (Google AdMob) to do so. See "Advertising" below for how information is handled when ads are served.

## Advertising

- The free version of the App shows Google AdMob rewarded ads (ads that add time when you finish watching them) only when you choose to add more time after using up the daily time for playing two videos together.
- To serve ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and the App. This information is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  - [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- In the EU, the UK and some other regions, the App first asks for your consent to ad serving using Google's consent management tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from Settings → About this app → Privacy settings in the App.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- If you purchase Pro (one-time purchase), no ads are shown and the advertising SDK does not run.

## Data Handling on Your Device

- Videos you select are read locally on your device only. They are never sent to external servers.
- Saved comparisons, previous session states and the record of daily time for playing videos together are stored in the app's data area on your device. This data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
- Your choices about consent to ad serving are stored on your device by Google's consent management tool.
- Exported videos and images are saved only to the destination you choose (e.g., your gallery or a sharing target).

## In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment information (such as credit card numbers).
