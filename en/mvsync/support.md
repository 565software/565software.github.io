# Support

## FAQ

### After switching phones or reinstalling, Premium went back to the free version

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used for the purchase, then tap Settings → About this app → Restore purchase in MVsync. Once the purchase is found, you'll have Premium again.

### I want a refund

Purchases of MVsync Premium are processed by Google Play, so refunds are requested through Google Play. Please follow the steps in the Google Play Help.

- [Learn about Google Play refund policies (Google Play Help)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en)
